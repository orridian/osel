## Origin of the mission

Developers and researchers who evaluate software, models and data pipelines often cannot answer simple questions later:
*Which exact inputs produced this number? Did the change actually improve anything, or is it noise? Can someone else rerun it?*
OSEL was founded to build small, dependable, local-first tools that make those answers routine.

## Initial problem

Evaluation results are often produced by ad-hoc scripts. The dataset version, configuration, metric code version and environment
are not recorded, so results cannot be compared or reproduced, and regressions slip through CI.

## Initial scope

1. **Product A — `osel` evaluation toolkit** (alpha, in development): define an evaluation, run it, record the exact inputs and
   configuration, compare against a baseline, and fail CI on regressions.
2. Product B and Product C are **roadmap hypotheses only** (see [VISION.md](VISION.md)), gated by the expansion rule in
   [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md#expansion-gate).
