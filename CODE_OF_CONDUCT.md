# Code of Conduct

| Owner | Review date | Reporting channel |
|---|---|---|
| Conduct contacts (currently the Founder, with a trusted adult as alternate) | Every 6 months; next: May 2027 | `orridian.contact@gmail.com` · alternate: [conduct-alternate-contact] |

## 1. Scope

This Code applies in OSEL repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, events (online and in person), and any official OSEL activity. It also applies to people representing OSEL elsewhere.

## 2. Standards

We expect:

- respectful technical disagreement, aimed at ideas rather than people;
- constructive, specific feedback;
- inclusive participation, including patience with newcomers and people working in a second language;
- no harassment or threats.

## 3. Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, including repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks based on identity or background.
- Doxxing: publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Intimidation, threats, or advocating violence.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, events or project work.

## 4. Reporting

Report privately to `orridian.contact@gmail.com` (subject line: "Conduct report"). If the primary contact is involved in the incident, use the alternate route: [conduct-alternate-contact]. It goes to a trusted adult who is not the primary contact.
You may report on behalf of someone else. Reports in public issues will be moved to private handling.

## 5. Response

1. Acknowledge the report (best-effort target: 7 days; OSEL is run by a volunteer, so if you hear nothing in 7 days, use the alternate contact).
2. Assess immediate safety and take interim steps if needed.
3. Preserve relevant records (links, screenshots, logs).
4. Investigate fairly, hearing from those involved where safe.
5. Decide on a proportionate outcome and inform the reporter and the subject.

## 6. Confidentiality

Information is shared only with people who need it to respond. We will protect reporters' identities as far as possible, but **we cannot promise absolute secrecy**, for example where safety or law requires disclosure.

## 7. Enforcement

Outcomes are proportionate and may include:

- a private warning;
- a public warning;
- temporary restriction from participating;
- removal from an event or the community;
- other proportionate measures.

## 8. Review

Decisions are documented in a private conduct log. The affected person may ask for reconsideration once, with new information, to a conduct contact not involved in the original decision.
