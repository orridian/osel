# Records and retention policy

**Owner:** Founder · **Review:** yearly · **Channel:** `orridian.contact@gmail.com` · **Enforcement:** quarterly audit (see [operations/cadence.md](../operations/cadence.md)).

| Record | Why kept | Access | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Git history, releases, public issues | Project history and evidence | Public | Indefinite |
| Decision log | Accountability | Public (sensitive detail omitted) | Indefinite |
| Founder evidence archive | Proof of founding and role history | Founder + guardian | Indefinite; personal data minimized |
| Metric ledger | Evidence for reported metrics | Maintainers | 3 years, then aggregated |
| Alpha-tester / interview notes | Product discovery | Maintainers | 24 months; pseudonymized after 6 months |
| Email correspondence | Replying, continuity | Mailbox handlers | 24 months after last message |
| Conduct reports | Fair handling, patterns | Conduct contacts only | 3 years after resolution |
| Security reports | Coordinated disclosure | Security contacts only | 3 years after advisory |
| Event registrations | Running the event | Organizer | 30 days after event (accessibility notes), 12 months (names/emails) |

Deletion is logged (what, when, who), never with the deleted content itself.
