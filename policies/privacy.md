# Privacy policy

**Owner:** Founder, reviewed by a parent/guardian or qualified adult · **Last reviewed:** 24 Nov 2026 (approved by parent/guardian) · **Contact:** `orridian.contact@gmail.com` · **Enforcement:** any change that collects new personal data must update this policy first, or it is reverted.

> This policy describes **actual current practice**. Re-check it against the real website and tools before every website launch or change.

## What we collect

| System | Personal data collected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `osel` software | **None.** It runs locally, has no telemetry and makes no network calls. | — |
| osel website | **None by OSEL.** No analytics, cookies, or forms. The static host may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent) for security. | Security and operation by the host |
| GitHub repositories | Whatever you choose to post publicly on GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy terms | Collaboration |
| Email to role addresses | Your email address and message | To reply to you |

## Legal basis (where applicable)

Replying to messages you send us is based on legitimate interest or your request. If OSEL ever needs consent-based processing, this policy will be updated before it starts.

## Processors

The static hosting provider and email forwarding provider will be listed here by name once they are chosen.

## Retention

Emails are kept for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted. See [records-retention.md](records-retention.md).

## Security

Role addresses use forwarding/aliases. Access is limited to the people who handle the relevant mailbox.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your correspondence by writing to the contact address. We will not add you to any mailing list without your explicit opt-in.

## Changes

Changes are made by pull request, so the full history of this policy is public in Git.
