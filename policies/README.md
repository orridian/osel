# OSEL policy index

A policy is complete only when it reflects actual practice and has an owner, a review date, a reporting channel and an enforcement process.
Status words mean exactly this: **Adopted** = reflects current practice and in force. **Proposed** = text ready, waiting for review/approval, not in force. **Draft** = written in advance, not in force until its trigger occurs and it is reviewed. **Operational once…** = text final, but the channel it relies on must work first.
Policies involving legal obligations, privacy, contracts or regulated data need review by a parent/guardian and, where needed, a qualified professional **before adoption**.
