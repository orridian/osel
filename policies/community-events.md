# Community event policy — DRAFT (adopt before first public event)

**Owner:** Community organizer (Founder until appointed) · **Channel:** `orridian.contact@gmail.com`; at events, the named on-site/online moderator · **Enforcement:** Code of Conduct procedures apply.

- **Registration data:** collect only name/handle and contact email. Optional accessibility needs are deleted 30 days after the event. No dates of birth or addresses.
- **Minors:** events involving under-18 participants have an adult supervisor present, and follow the host venue's safeguarding rules.
- **Recording and photography:** announced in advance. Participants can opt out (camera off, a lanyard colour, or being left out of photos). Recordings are published only with speaker consent.
- **Moderation:** at least one named moderator per event who is not presenting. The Code of Conduct is linked in the registration page and the opening slide.
- **Accessibility:** captions or a transcript where feasible. Slides shared in advance. Time zones stated. Breaks scheduled.
- **Participant safety:** no sharing of participants' contact details. Meeting links are not posted publicly for online events with minors.
- **Follow-up:** materials are shared. Nobody is added to a mailing list without opting in.
