# Conflict of interest policy — DRAFT (not in force)

**Trigger:** when external decision-makers (maintainers, advisors, reviewers) join. **Owner:** Founder · **Channel:** private message to the Founding Technical Lead · **Enforcement:** undisclosed conflicts can lead to decisions being reopened and permissions reviewed.

## What to disclose

Financial or personal interests that could affect, or appear to affect:

- code or benchmark reviews (e.g. you work on a competing tool);
- procurement or spending decisions;
- governance decisions;
- research conclusions (funding sources, affiliations).

## How

Disclose in the relevant issue or PR, or privately to the Founding Technical Lead if sensitive. Disclosures are recorded in the decision log (sensitive detail omitted).

## Effect

A conflicted person may comment but does not approve or decide. Another maintainer decides. If no one else can decide, the conflict is stated publicly next to the decision.
