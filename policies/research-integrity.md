# Research integrity policy — DRAFT (not in force)

**Trigger:** before formal research collaborations. **Owner:** Research lead (Founder until appointed) · **Channel:** `research@[osel-domain]` · **Enforcement:** outputs that don't meet this policy are not published under the OSEL name; published errors are corrected publicly.

1. **Reproducibility.** Code, configs and exact software versions are published. Data is published, or referenced where licensing forbids redistribution.
2. **Data provenance.** Every dataset records source, version or hash, license and any filtering or exclusions.
3. **Protocol first.** Main experiments follow a protocol written before running them ([template](../programs/templates/research-protocol.md)). Deviations are reported.
4. **Preregistration** is used when the conclusions are confirmatory and the field expects it.
5. **Negative and inconclusive results** are reported, not dropped.
6. **Authorship** reflects substantive intellectual contribution, agreed in writing early. Acknowledgments cover the rest.
7. **Citation.** Prior work and the tools used are cited accurately. No fabricated references.
8. **Conflicts** are disclosed under the conflict of interest policy.
9. **Result labels:** *exploratory*, *confirmed* (protocol followed), or *independently reproduced* (by someone outside the original team).
