## Quarterly aggregation

1. Filter rows by period and `Counted? = yes`.
2. Count distinct subjects per metric.
3. Write the number, method and limitations into the [impact report](templates/quarterly-impact-report.md).
4. A second person re-counts one metric from this ledger as a spot check.
