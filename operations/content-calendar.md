# Launch content calendar (first 6 weeks after the alpha)

Every piece must be useful on its own, even to readers who never adopt osel. Sources are cited, limitations are stated, and there are no adoption claims without evidence (METRICS.md).

| Week | Topic | Format | Source material | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why OSEL exists and the problem it addresses | Blog post | `website/src/pages/blog/why-osel.md` + `website/drafts/alpha-announcement.md` | Website, GitHub Discussions |
| 2 | A complete evaluation workflow with osel | Tutorial + 75-second video | `examples/demo-repo/`, `programs/demo-video-script.md` | Website, relevant developer communities (personalized, not mass-posted) |
| 3 | How osel identifies datasets and records runs | Technical article | `examples/demos/01-reproducibility/`, `website/drafts/architecture.md` | Website |
| 4 | Comparing runs: why an average alone misleads | Technical article | `examples/demos/03-uncertainty/` | Website; share with research/student contacts |
| 5 | Gating pull requests on evaluations with GitHub Actions | Tutorial | `examples/demos/02-regression/`, `integrations/github-action/` | Website, GitHub Discussions |
| 6 | What the first alpha testers found, and what changed | Honest retrospective | Early-access tracker (aggregated, anonymized, with consent) | Website |

## Rules

- Publish only after the content's commands have been rerun against the current release.
- University and research outreach uses a specific use case and the one-page brief (`programs/university-engagement.md`), never a generic "partner with us" request.
- If a week slips because of school, the calendar slips. Don't cram (`time-budget.md`).
