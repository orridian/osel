# Risk register

Reviewed monthly; re-scored quarterly. Likelihood and impact: L/M/H.

| Risk | Early warning | Mitigation | L | I | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wrong product problem | Interviews produce compliments but no concrete pain or trial users | More discovery; narrow or change the problem; gate G1 | M | H | Founder | Open — discovery in progress |
| Too much scope | Many modules, no end-to-end workflow | Freeze features; prioritize the core user journey | M | H | Founder | Mitigated — end-to-end workflow done before extras |
| No distribution | Product exists but few qualified users find it | Useful technical proof; relevant integrations; targeted outreach | H | H | Founder | Open |
| Low retention | People install once and don't return | Interview non-returning testers; improve value and onboarding | M | M | Founder | Not yet measurable |
| Founder bottleneck | All reviews, releases and decisions wait on the founder | Document processes (done); recruit and train maintainers | H | M | Founder | Open |
| Contributor churn | New contributors get no response or unclear tasks | Issue quality standard; review cadence; onboarding | M | M | Founder | Mitigated by templates; untested |
| Security incident | Secrets committed, vulnerable dependency, unsafe data handling | Secret scanning, least privilege, private reporting, response plan, redaction tests | L | H | Security contact | Mitigated |
| Metric inflation | Downloads/stars presented as active use or partnerships | Written definitions, evidence ledger, independent review | M | H | Founder | Mitigated by METRICS.md |
| Burnout / school conflict | Sleep, school, health or relationships deteriorate | Reduce scope, delegate, pause nonessential programs. **Do not increase hours as the default** | M | H | Founder + guardian | Open — see [time-budget.md](time-budget.md) |
| Legal/administrative issue | Contracts, payments, data agreements or registration handled informally | Involve parent/guardian and a qualified professional before any commitment | L | H | Founder + guardian | Mitigated by GOVERNANCE.md |
| Name conflict | Existing project or trademark uses "OSEL" | Check before domain/branding spend | M | M | Founder | Open — check pending |
