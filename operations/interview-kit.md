# Discovery interview kit

## Interviewee list (keep the filled-in version PRIVATE)

Sources: people who have **publicly** written about evaluation pain (blog posts, issues, talks, forum threads). Record only what is needed to make contact and to remember why.

Don't collect private emails, phone numbers, ages, locations or employer details beyond what is needed.

## Outreach message (personalize every one; low pressure)

> Hi [name], I read your [post/issue] about [specific problem]. I'm building a small open-source tool around evaluation reproducibility and I'm trying to understand the problem before building more. Would you be open to a 20-minute call sometime in the next few weeks? Nothing to buy and no pitch. If not, no worries at all.

A parent/guardian should know about scheduled calls with adults. Prefer public or recorded-with-consent video calls; never share a home address.

## Script (20 min)

1. Permission: "May I take notes? I'll keep them private and only use quotes with your permission."
2. "Tell me about the last time you evaluated a change to a model, prompt or pipeline."
3. "How did you decide whether it was better?" (listen for: noise, datasets, CI)
4. "Has a result ever been hard to reproduce or compare later? What happened? What did it cost?"
5. "What do you use today? What do you like or dislike about it?"
6. "If that problem disappeared tomorrow, what would change for you?"
7. Optional: show the quick start only at the end. "Would you try this on a real workflow?"
8. "Who else should I talk to?"

Avoid leading questions and pitching. Compliments are not evidence. Specific past behavior is.

## After each call

Write notes within 24 hours (privately, using the pseudonym). Tag which [candidate problems](user-problems.md) were confirmed or rejected, with quotes.
