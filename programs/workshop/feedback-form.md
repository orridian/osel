# Workshop feedback form

Anonymous by default. Optional fields are clearly marked. Responses are deleted after 12 months.

1. Which exercises did you complete? ☐ 1 ☐ 2 ☐ 3
2. What worked well?
3. Where did you get stuck? (step + what happened)
4. How useful was this for your own work? (1–5)
5. What one thing should we change?
6. *(Optional)* May we follow up about your answers? If yes, leave a contact. It is used only for this follow-up and never added to a mailing list.
